## Professional statement

My approach to cybersecurity is grounded in a question: **how can an organisation turn technical information into decisions that meaningfully reduce risk?**

I bring a background in performance management, financial control, project delivery and business transformation, including establishing performance management capability within an international banking subsidiary. I am building on that experience through structured cybersecurity training, hands-on learning environments and scenario-based projects.

I am particularly interested in the space where **security operations, governance, risk and compliance (GRC), technology assurance and operational resilience** meet. I enjoy examining how controls are designed, how their effectiveness can be evidenced, and how teams can communicate uncertainty and priorities to decision-makers.

My aim is to combine analytical thinking, clear communication and practical security knowledge to help organisations understand their exposure, respond proportionately and strengthen their resilience over time.

## Evidence and responsible use

I aim to make the evidence behind each project clear and easy to understand:

- **Authentic lab captures** are identified as such and redacted where appropriate.
- **Simulated examples and reconstructed images** are labelled as illustrative; they do not represent captured lab results.
- Fictional organisations, findings and business outcomes are identified as scenario material.
- A tool alert, open port or technical score is treated as a starting point for investigation—not proof of compromise or business impact.
- Sensitive information, credentials and unauthorised target details are excluded from public material.
