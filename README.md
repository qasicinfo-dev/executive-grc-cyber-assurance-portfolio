## Evidence and responsible use

I aim to make the evidence behind each project clear and easy to understand:

- **Authentic lab captures** are identified as such and redacted where appropriate.
- **Simulated examples and reconstructed images** are labelled as illustrative; they do not represent captured lab results.
- Fictional organisations, findings and business outcomes are identified as scenario material.
- A tool alert, open port or technical score is treated as a starting point for investigation—not proof of compromise or business impact.
- Sensitive information, credentials and unauthorised target details are excluded from public material.
