# Fictional board assurance paper | Northstar Payments Ltd

## Decision requested

Approve a 12-month, risk-based control-improvement programme for the payments-support service. Begin with critical host-hardening evidence, firewall-governance and alert-triage gaps. Delegate progress oversight to the Technology Risk Committee and escalate exceptions above appetite.

## Current position

| Dimension | Fictional position | Leadership implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Host configuration | Baseline evidence is incomplete or inconsistently retained. | Assurance cannot prove consistent hardening without an evidence standard. |
| Firewall governance | Rule state is visible but review/approval trail needs strengthening. | Unnecessary access may remain after changes. |
| Alert handling | Alerts exist; ownership, severity and closure evidence need formalisation. | Material alerts may not reach an accountable decision-maker in time. |

## Options

| Option | Benefit | Trade-off | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Remediate all gaps immediately | Reduces uncertainty fastest. | High delivery demand and potential service disruption. | Use only for exposures beyond delegated tolerance. |
| Phase by critical-service exposure | Protects the most material services while managing change risk. | Requires disciplined governance and interim controls. | **Recommended.** |
| Time-bound acceptance | Avoids immediate change/cost. | Builds control debt and weakens audit/resilience confidence. | Only with named approval and expiry. |

## 90-day plan

1. Confirm critical-service inventory and owners.
2. Agree hardening, firewall-review and alert-triage control standards.
3. Create an evidence register, test calendar, action owners and escalation rules.
4. Remediate highest-exposure gaps and document interim safeguards.
5. Establish monthly committee reporting on evidence coverage, aged actions and residual risk.

## Measures

- Critical-service control-evidence coverage.
- High-risk action ageing and time-bound acceptances.
- Firewall-review completion.
- Alert triage within agreed decision window.
- Repeat control finding rate.
