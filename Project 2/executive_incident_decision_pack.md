# Fictional executive incident decision pack | Harbour Mutual

## Decision requested

Approve proportionate containment and heightened incident governance while investigation continues. Confirm the accountable executive, decision authority, customer/regulatory decision points and update cadence. This is a fictional exercise; it does not represent a real incident.

## Executive situation report

| Question | Fictional position |
| --- | --- |
| What is known? | A suspicious email, a recorded link click and network activity requiring investigation. |
| What is not known? | Whether credentials were compromised, whether an endpoint executed harmful content, and whether the events are related. |
| Customer/service impact | No confirmed disruption or data impact at this stage; impact assessment remains open. |
| Current classification | Security event under investigation, not a confirmed incident. |
| Immediate priority | Preserve evidence, validate scope and make proportionate containment decisions. |

## Decision options

| Option | Benefit | Trade-off | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Isolate the affected endpoint and reset relevant credentials immediately | Limits potential spread and credential misuse. | May interrupt a user/service and may affect evidence handling. | Recommended where approved response criteria are met. |
| Increase monitoring while investigation continues | Preserves business continuity while gaining information. | Delays containment if compromise is later confirmed. | Use only with explicit risk owner and time limit. |
| Declare a major incident immediately | Accelerates mobilisation and oversight. | Can create unnecessary operational/legal escalation if evidence is weak. | Trigger only when threshold is met. |

## Governance and communications

| Role | Accountability |
| --- | --- |
| Incident Commander | Directs response and records operational decisions. |
| Executive Risk Owner | Accepts material risk and authorises decisions beyond operational delegation. |
| Legal/Privacy lead | Assesses notification, contractual and privacy implications. |
| Communications lead | Coordinates customer, colleague and external messaging. |
| Board/Risk Committee | Receives material escalation, assurance and post-incident lessons. |

## 90-day resilience plan

1. Exercise the phishing-to-identity-compromise response playbook.
2. Define alert-to-decision service-level targets and escalation thresholds.
3. Improve evidence retention, incident logging and handover quality.
4. Tune detections and verify false-positive/false-negative lessons.
5. Report recurring root causes, action ageing and recovery readiness to the appropriate forum.

## Executive measures

- Time from material alert to named accountable decision.
- Percentage of significant events with complete evidence and decision records.
- Time to contain and restore a critical fictional service during exercises.
- Completion and effectiveness of post-incident actions.
- Repeat events associated with previously accepted root causes.
