# Fictional executive incident decision paper | Harbour Mutual

## Decision requested

Approve proportionate containment and heightened incident governance while investigation continues. Confirm the accountable executive, decision authority, update cadence and escalation threshold. This is a fictional exercise, not a real incident.

## Executive situation report

| Question | Fictional position |
| --- | --- |
| What is known? | Suspicious email, link click and network activity requiring investigation. |
| What is unknown? | Whether credentials were compromised, harmful content executed or events are related. |
| Customer/service impact | No confirmed disruption or data impact; assessment remains open. |
| Classification | Security event under investigation, not a confirmed incident. |
| Immediate priority | Preserve evidence, validate scope and decide proportionate containment. |

## Decision options

| Option | Benefit | Trade-off | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Isolate endpoint/reset credentials where criteria are met | Reduces potential misuse and spread. | May disrupt service and affect evidence handling. | Recommended where approved criteria are satisfied. |
| Heighten monitoring while investigation continues | Preserves continuity while gathering context. | Delays containment if compromise is later confirmed. | Only with named risk owner and time limit. |
| Declare a major incident | Mobilises oversight quickly. | May create unnecessary escalation if evidence is weak. | Trigger only at the defined threshold. |

## 90-day resilience programme

1. Exercise the phishing-to-identity-compromise response playbook.
2. Agree alert-to-decision target, authority matrix and update cadence.
3. Improve evidence retention, incident records and handover quality.
4. Tune detection and record false-positive/false-negative lessons.
5. Report root causes, action ageing and recovery readiness to the Risk Committee.
