# Executive portfolio infographics

These original infographics are authorised-lab portfolio visual summaries. They use fictional organisation context and do not depict real clients, live systems or production metrics.

1. **Executive Cyber Control Assurance:** shows how technical lab evidence becomes control testing, risk treatment and Board/Committee assurance.
2. **Executive Incident Decision & Resilience:** shows how an alert is validated and turned into a proportionate, accountable response and a resilience-improvement loop.
3. **Executive Vulnerability Governance:** shows how CVSS, asset criticality, exposure and controls support a risk-based remediation and investment decision.
